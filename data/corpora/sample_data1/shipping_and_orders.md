# Shipping & Orders

## Roasting cutoff

Aurora roasts to order in small batches. If your order is placed before 10:00
AM Pacific Time on a weekday, it will be roasted the same day and ship the
following business day. Orders placed after the 10:00 AM Pacific cutoff, or on
a weekend, are roasted on the next roast day instead.

## Shipping cost

Standard shipping is a flat $6 per order. Orders over $40 qualify for free
standard shipping. Expedited shipping is available at checkout for an
additional fee and is not eligible for the free-shipping threshold.

## International shipping

Aurora currently does not ship outside the United States. We hope to expand
international shipping in the future, but for now all orders must have a
delivery address within the U.S.

## Subscriptions

A coffee subscription can be paused or cancelled at any time from the
customer account page with no fee. Subscription orders follow the same
roasting cutoff and shipping rules as one-time orders.
