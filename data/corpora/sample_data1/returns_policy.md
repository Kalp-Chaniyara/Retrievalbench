# Returns & Refunds

Because coffee is a perishable food product, Aurora does not accept returns
of opened bags. If a bag arrives damaged or you received the wrong item,
contact support within 14 days of delivery for a free replacement or full
refund. Aurora does not require the damaged item to be shipped back.

Unopened bags may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a refund of the
item price; return shipping is the customer's responsibility in that case.

## Exchanges

Aurora does not offer direct exchanges. If you ordered the wrong roast or
grind, request a refund for the unopened bag (see above) and place a new
order for the item you actually wanted. Exchanges on opened bags are not
possible for the same food-safety reason returns aren't accepted.

## Damaged or missing brewing equipment

Non-coffee items — the pour-over dripper, filters, and any other brewing
equipment — follow a 30-day return window instead of the 14-day coffee
window, since they are not perishable. Equipment can be returned opened or
unopened as long as it is in resalable condition; contact support to start
an equipment return.

## Gift orders

If you're returning or requesting a refund on an order that was sent as a
gift, the refund is issued to the original purchaser's payment method, not
to the gift recipient. The recipient's shipping address is never shared
with the purchaser as part of a return request.

## How to start a return or refund

Email support with your order number, and mention whether the issue is
damage, a wrong item, or an unopened-bag return. Support typically responds
within one business day and will issue a prepaid label if a physical return
is required (equipment only — coffee bags are never shipped back).
