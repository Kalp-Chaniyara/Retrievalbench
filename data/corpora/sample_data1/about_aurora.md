# About Aurora

Aurora Coffee Roasters was founded in 2014 in Portland, Oregon by Mara Velez
and Tomas Idris. Mara Velez is the CEO of the company.

Aurora's mission is to make single-origin specialty coffee accessible
without sacrificing traceability. Every bag of coffee Aurora sells lists the
farm of origin, the harvest year, and the name of the importer.

Aurora has been a certified B Corporation since 2021.

## Sourcing

Aurora pays a minimum of $3.50 per pound to producers, which is well above
the Fair Trade floor price. Every producer relationship is direct-trade,
renegotiated each harvest year rather than locked into a long-term contract.
