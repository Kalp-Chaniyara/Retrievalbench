# Brewing Guide

## Equipment

Aurora sells a stainless steel pour-over dripper for $28 and a set of
unbleached paper filters for $9.

## Ratio

For a 12-ounce cup, add about 22 grams of coffee to 350 grams of water. Scale
the ratio proportionally for larger or smaller cups.

## Water temperature

Use water between 195 and 205 degrees Fahrenheit. Boiling water at 212
degrees is too hot and can scorch the grounds, pulling out bitter flavors.

## Storage

We recommend you do not store coffee in the refrigerator or freezer for
daily use — condensation from repeated temperature changes degrades the
beans. Instead, keep coffee in an airtight container away from light, heat,
and moisture, and buy only what you'll use within a few weeks.
